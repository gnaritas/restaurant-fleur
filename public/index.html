Le Concept

A French-trained kitchen, Arkansas-grown

Chef Jennifer Maune — an Arkansas native, a MasterChef Season 13 finalist, and an École Ducasse Expert Diploma holder — is opening her flagship restaurant with her husband and co-founder Brian Maune. Expect elevated French-American cooking for lunch and dinner, chef's tasting menus, and produce-driven, garden-to-table plates.

La Maison

The restored Justice Building

Fleur takes over the ground floor and basement of the Bob R. Brooks Jr. Justice Building — the former Boyle Building, built in 1909 and brought back through a $35M renovation — at 101 W Capitol Ave, the corner of Capitol Avenue and Main Street.

Before you visit

When does Restaurant Fleur open?

Opening soon. Opening dates and reservation details will be announced on the restaurant's Instagram, @chefjennifermaune, and on this page.

Where exactly is it?

On the ground floor and basement of the Bob R. Brooks Jr. Justice Building — the former Boyle Building at 101 W Capitol Ave at Main Street, Little Rock, AR 72201.

What kind of food?

Elevated French-American lunch and dinner, chef's tasting menus, and garden-to-table cooking from Chef Jennifer Maune's kitchen.

Is there a full menu yet?

The menu has not been published yet — follow @chefjennifermaune for the first look.
