A 1909 landmark, restored

Restaurant Fleur takes the ground floor and basement of the Bob R. Brooks Jr. Justice Building — the former Boyle Building at Capitol Avenue and Main Street, back in service after a $35M renovation.

The building

The Boyle Building rose in 1909 and spent decades as one of downtown Little Rock's landmarks. After a $35M renovation, it returns as the Bob R. Brooks Jr. Justice Building — and the ground floor and basement become home to Restaurant Fleur, Fleur Pastry & Café, and Fleur Provisions.

The corner

Fleur sits at 101 W Capitol Ave, at Capitol Avenue and Main Street — downtown's spine, a few blocks from the state capitol. Directions, and answers to any question you have while the dining room takes shape, are on the contact page.
