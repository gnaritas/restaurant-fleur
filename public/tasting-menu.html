Chef's Tasting Menu

The long table

At the center of Restaurant Fleur: chef's tasting menus composed by Jennifer Maune — courses that follow her training at the École Ducasse and her Arkansas garden sourcing, from the kitchen of her flagship restaurant.

Who is composing it

Chef Jennifer Maune grew up in Arkansas, finished as the runner-up of MasterChef Season 13, and earned an Expert Diploma at the École Ducasse — the French hospitality school founded by Alain Ducasse. Restaurant Fleur, opened with co-founder Brian Maune, is where that cooking comes home: French technique applied to ingredients grown and raised in Arkansas, served as chef's tasting menus alongside à la carte lunch and dinner.

What to expect

A composed, multi-course menu that changes with the garden. The full course list has not been published yet — opening dates and the first menu reveal will come through the restaurant's channels as the kitchen readies the dining room.
