Privacy

How this site treats your information

Who runs this site

This website represents Restaurant Fleur, opening soon at 101 W Capitol Ave, Little Rock, AR 72201. It carries no advertising and no analytics.

The contact form

If you use the contact form, you send us your name, an optional phone number, an optional email address, and your message. We use it only to answer you, and we keep it only as long as needed to do that. We do not sell it, and we do not add you to marketing lists.

No trackers, no third-party scripts

This site sets no cookies and loads no third-party JavaScript or tracking pixels. Styles and icons are served by the site itself. On pages that show the location map, the embedded map (Google Maps) may set its own cookies under Google's privacy policy once it loads.

Your choices

Ask us to delete or correct anything you sent through the form the same way — through the contact form. If this policy changes, the change appears on this page.
